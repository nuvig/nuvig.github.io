Alerts

One bar per alert, on the day's clock — the meteogram's hours, widened only for an alert that began before this day or ran past it. Ticks are issuances: a watch updated three times is one bar with four ticks, not four rows. The dotted run-in is the lead time between the first issuance and onset. Night is shaded; a date instead of an hour marks midnight. Open a row for every issuance and its text.

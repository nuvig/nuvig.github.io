Data Feed

Every record this site takes in, newest first: METARs from KDCA, KNAK and the nearby fields, TAFs, forecast discussions, grid, forecast and model snapshots, alerts, and tracker snapshot pushes. Times are Eastern. Click a line for the whole record and the file it came from. Everything here is read from the weather archive and the traffic-data branch; this page collects nothing itself.

reading the archive…

Times. A grid, forecast, model, alert or tracker row is stamped when the site captured it. A metar, taf or afd row shows the observation or issuance time instead; the hourly archiver picked it up later. Those times have a dotted underline.

Sizes. Each line shows the size of the record behind it. One-liners leave part of it out: a METAR's remarks, a TAF's transmission header, a snapshot's hourly values. The expansion shows the whole record, or says where it was cut and links the file.

Coverage. Each day header shows how many hours of the day each METAR station has an observation for, how many hours are missing, and how many the station did not report. It also counts records filled in later from IEM rather than captured live.

The archive is written hourly by the wxarchive Action. Tracker snapshots are pushed by the Pi exporter. Pipeline health is on the changelog; the same days are on the almanac.
